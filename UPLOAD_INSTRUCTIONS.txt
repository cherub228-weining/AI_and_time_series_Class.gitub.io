QUICK SETUP - GITHUB WEB UPLOAD
================================

Target repository:
https://github.com/cherub228-weining/AI_and_time_series_Class.gitub.io

1. Extract AI_and_time_series_Class.gitub.io-upload.zip on your computer.

2. Open the extracted folder. Select all of its CONTENTS, including:
   .github, assets, data, downloads, index.html, styles.css, script.js,
   README.md, LICENSE.md, .gitignore and .nojekyll.

3. Open GitHub's upload page:
   https://github.com/cherub228-weining/AI_and_time_series_Class.gitub.io/upload

4. Drag the selected contents onto the upload page. Do not upload the ZIP
   itself, because GitHub will not unpack it.

5. At the bottom, use the commit message "Add paper companion site" and
   click "Commit changes".

6. Open the repository's Settings > Pages. Under Build and deployment,
   choose "GitHub Actions" as the source.

7. The included workflow will publish the site at:
   https://cherub228-weining.github.io/AI_and_time_series_Class.gitub.io/

UPDATING THE LITERATURE
-----------------------
Edit data/references.json. Copy an existing entry, update its fields, and
change lastUpdated. The page search and topic filters update automatically.

ADDING CLASS VIDEOS
-------------------
The five video slots are prepared in index.html under the section with
id="lectures". Add the recording URLs when they become available.
